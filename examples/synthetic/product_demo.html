Evidence and provenance

What the evidence establishes

Physical hazard and exposure can be geolocated and linked to operational or financial transmission channels.

What it does not establish

Flood depth or heat exposure alone does not determine damage, downtime, default probability, collateral haircut or insured loss.

What data would change the answer?

What is the ground-floor and critical-equipment elevation?
What are shift-worker counts and operating hours?
Which primary and alternative routes connect the site to its actual logistics endpoint?
What cooling, ventilation, drinking-water and backup-power arrangements exist?

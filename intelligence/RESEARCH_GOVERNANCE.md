# Research-aware intelligence governance

## Core rule
Research can justify a transmission channel or a calculation method. It does not automatically
justify importing a coefficient into Bangladesh, into another sector, or into an individual asset.

## Evidence-use classes
- `DESIGN_AND_MECHANISM_SUPPORT`: can shape architecture and analytical questions.
- `MECHANISM_SUPPORT`: supports a causal/operational channel, but not a numerical loss coefficient.
- `REGULATORY_SCENARIO_REFERENCE`: retain as an official sensitivity parameter only.
- `METHOD_RESEARCH`: candidate model architecture requiring local calibration.
- `CONTEXT_AND_SPATIAL_DESIGN`: macro/national evidence useful for prioritisation.
- `DO_NOT_USE_FOR_NUMERIC_CALIBRATION`: evidence is methodologically unsuitable, superseded,
  retracted or otherwise blocked from numerical calibration.

## Transferability
Every research item is tagged:
- direct Bangladesh;
- Bangladesh sector-specific;
- South Asian/regional analogue;
- global/transferrable method.

No external coefficient is applied automatically when geography, sector, worker setting, building
type, hazard definition or outcome differ materially.

## Research status
A future `research_status` field should support:
`ACTIVE`, `UPDATED`, `SUPERSEDED`, `RETRACTED_UNDERLYING`, `RETRACTED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`.

Any indicator that depends on a non-ACTIVE calibration must be invalidated or regenerated.

## Narrative rule
The product may say:
- "this site has 1,500 workers and 24 extreme-heat days";
- "this equals 36,000 worker-heat exposure days";
- "Bangladesh and RMG-specific research indicates heat strain/productivity is a relevant mechanism."

It may NOT say:
- "36,000 exposure days cause 4,320 lost workdays"
unless a specifically validated production-loss function is documented and approved.

## Customer data rule
Third-order financial analysis becomes materially stronger when customers add:
- bank EAD/outstanding balance;
- collateral value and LTV;
- insurance coverage/sum insured;
- revenue/export value;
- raw-material and buyer dependencies;
- shift/workforce schedules;
- water source/consumption;
- backup power and cooling;
- floor/equipment elevation;
- route/port dependencies.

These data remain customer-specific overlays; they are not duplicated into public Mangrove source tables.
